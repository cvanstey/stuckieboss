I’ve spent the last few years building projects that connect archival materials to modern mapping tools — restoring historical maps, creating archival datasets, and automating workflows. My work sits at the intersection of data science, digital preservation, and public history.

Currently an AI Fellow and graduate student in Data Science, I bring a background in American Studies and Literature. I’m especially interested in GIS and have tested both QGIS and ArcGIS extensively — tools central to my recent work.

I thrive in remote, cross-disciplinary environments where clarity, autonomy, and collaboration matter. I’m passionate about making historical data more accessible through thoughtful, community-driven technology projects.
